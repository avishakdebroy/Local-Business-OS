What a linked phone can do

  • Photograph a barcode — the computer reads the number and finds the product.
  • Photograph your notebook page or a supplier bill — it goes to the Review screen on the computer.
  • See today's figures — read only.

A phone cannot change the books, remove entries, or reach the backup and settings screens. Those stay on this computer.
